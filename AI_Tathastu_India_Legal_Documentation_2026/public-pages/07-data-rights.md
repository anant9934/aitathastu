## 1. Request help with your personal data
You may contact [VERIFY: email / privacy requests form] about the data that AI Tathastu holds in connection with your account. When the applicable statutory provisions are in force and apply, we support rights including access to information, correction, completion, updating, erasure, consent withdrawal and grievance redressal subject to lawful exceptions. Do not claim a general DPDP 'data portability' right unless another applicable law or product feature provides it.

## 2. Available request types
**Find my information:** ask about categories held, purposes and relevant sharing.  
**Correct or update:** correct a mistaken CV extraction, institution, result, profile link or accepted claim.  
**Erase:** request deletion of specified data or closure of your account subject to legal retention.  
**Withdraw permission:** stop future optional AI enrichment, a particular profile sync, marketing/analytics use or another authorized purpose as applicable.  
**Object to incorrect verified status:** ask for review of an evidence or verification outcome.  
**Complaint:** report mishandling, unexpected data sharing or inaccessible controls.

## 3. Identity/authority verification
We may request reasonable information to establish that the requester is the account holder or legally authorized parent/guardian. We do not request full identity documents unless necessary and lawful. Requests must not disclose another person's information to an unauthorized requester.

## 4. How requests are handled
After receiving a request we assess its scope, applicable law, existing permissions, technical records, backups, authorized recipients and any applicable exceptions. We will confirm receipt and provide a response within the legally applicable timeframe; the exact published service timeline must be verified by counsel and operationally supported. We will explain meaningful denials and the escalation process.

## 5. Withdrawal is not retroactive
Withdrawing a provider connection stops future retrieval under the withdrawn authorization. It cannot change copies already disclosed outside our control or undo completed external model inference. Deleting a raw CV does not necessarily delete accepted profile fields derived from it unless the request includes those fields.

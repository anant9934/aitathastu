### Prepare before incident
Assign incident owner, deputy, lawyer/privacy lead, contacts, monitoring pipeline, India-jurisdiction security logs where applicable, alert channels, provider incident support and clock/timezone handling. Store a secure evidence trail without exposing CV text unnecessarily. Evaluate CERT-In's six-hour reporting clock for reportable incidents and DPDP notification obligations when effective. Do not assume all faults or errors are statutory incidents.

### During an incident
1. Detect/triage, preserve relevant logs and identifiers, establish incident start/detection times.
2. Contain unauthorized access or active acquisition, isolate credentials, avoid deleting forensic evidence.
3. Assess affected data categories, number of users, minors, institutions, vendors and transfer regions.
4. Consult applicable notification triggers, deadlines and designated reporting authority.
5. Coordinate factual affected-user messages, remediation and recurrence prevention.
6. Document timeline, approvals, logs retained and lessons learned.

### Requests/complaints
Maintain a staffed contact, identity verification, request classification, response records, lawful exception assessment and escalation/appeal path. Do not invent a response SLA or statutory officer designation without checking the actual applicable provisions and operating capacity. Security and privacy complaints must be distinguishable from copyright and academic verification disputes.

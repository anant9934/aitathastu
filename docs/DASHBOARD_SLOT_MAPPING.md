# AI Tathastu — 5-Section Dashboard Slot Mapping

## 1. Overview
The AI Tathastu student dashboard follows a 5-section layout that adapts dynamically to the student's primary and secondary interests while preserving structural design lock.

---

## 2. Section 1: Overview
- **Hero Banner**: Displays greeting, target AI/ML role badge, active goal badge, and primary-priority personalized message.
- **EORA Insight Card**: Grounded conversational summary explaining what evidence exists and what is recommended next.
- **Summary Metric Grid (6 Slots)**:
  1. *Documented Skills*: Count of self-reported and verified competencies.
  2. *Active Projects*: Authoritative project records.
  3. *Evidence Items*: Total verifiable evidence points on the Person Graph.
  4. *Goal Readiness*: Defensible score (%) or "Provisional" status.
  5. *Network & Mentors*: Opt-in community status.
  6. *Roadmap Coverage*: Completed vs. remaining role prerequisites.

---

## 3. Section 2: Your Evidence
- **Persisted Projects**: Multi-technology project cards with source attribution (GitHub, manual entry).
- **GitHub Sync**: Direct synchronization status and verified timestamps when authorized.
- **Explainable Evidence**: Links each artifact to its supporting dimension without artificial inflated ratings.

---

## 4. Section 3: Your Growth
- **Competency Matrix**: Evaluates recorded skills against the 14 AI/ML role prerequisite definitions.
- **Personalized Roadmap**: Step-by-step milestone progression (prerequisite mastery, hands-on implementation, benchmark evaluation).

---

## 5. Section 4: Your Ecosystem
- **Jobs Primary**: Curated internship and junior role listings.
- **People Primary**: Verified faculty and alumni advisors.
- **Collaboration Primary**: Open-source repositories seeking contributors.
- **Research Primary**: SOTA paper preprints and virtual conferences.

---

## 6. Section 5: Next Best Actions
- **Top 5 Actions**: Ranked by goal alignment and primary interest weighting.
- **Explainability**: Each action provides explicit *Why am I seeing this?* reasoning.

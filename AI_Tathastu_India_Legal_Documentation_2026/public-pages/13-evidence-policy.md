## Claim statuses
**Detected:** a parser found text or a URL; accuracy unknown.  
**Suggested:** a rule or model proposed a field for review.  
**Student-confirmed:** the student explicitly accepted or corrected the field.  
**Source-supported:** linked evidence appears relevant but its authenticity or ownership may not be proven.  
**Independently verified:** a designated verifier completed an authorized review using the published policy.  
**Needs changes / Rejected:** review did not confirm the claim.

## What counts as evidence?
An uploaded resume, repository, project demo, certificate, academic record or publication may be submitted as evidence. Its existence alone does not establish competence, ownership, authorship, employment, school result or a completed certification. Evidence review must consider provenance, timestamps, relevance, duplicates and legitimate corrections. Reviewers should not verify their own claims where role controls prohibit that action.

## AI assistance
AI may suggest skill categories, text summaries, source relationships or learning modules, but must not silently mark a claim independently verified. A proposed skill proficiency level is not an assessment result. Scores or readiness labels must be explainable, policy versioned and based on real evidence rather than fabricated measurements.

## Students' controls
Students should be able to see the claim, its cited source, who/what proposed it, the review state and the process to correct, reject or appeal it. Institution/organization access is scope-limited. No guarantee of admission, grades, job placement or professional certification is made.

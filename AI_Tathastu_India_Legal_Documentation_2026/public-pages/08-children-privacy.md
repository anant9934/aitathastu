## 1. Who this applies to
This notice is for people under 18 and their parent or lawful guardian. AI Tathastu may be used in an educational setting and must not assume every student is an adult. Under India's DPDP framework, the relevant under-18 provisions have phased commencement; other current contractual and statutory rules can also apply.

## 2. Our intended safety approach
We aim to request only information needed for age-appropriate learning and account functions. Where legally required, covered processing will require verified parent/guardian permission before it starts. We will not treat a generic school/university affiliation or a minor's own checkbox as automatically satisfying such obligations. We will examine any narrow educational exceptions with counsel rather than claim a blanket exemption.

## 3. AI and third-party limits
Some model APIs, including the Gemini Developer API under its current terms, restrict availability for apps likely used by under-18 people. We must check each provider's age and data-use restrictions and withhold a provider rather than try to bypass its terms. No optional external AI data transfer occurs merely because a CV was uploaded.

## 4. Learning and profile features
We will review public sharing, recruiter/institution access, behavioral monitoring, personalized recommendations, analytics and profiling for child-specific legal restrictions. Student profile information should default to a restricted visibility state. Advertising and targeted marketing aimed at children must be separately assessed and restricted as law requires.

## 5. Parent/guardian authorization
The actual service must implement an age-appropriate verification method that collects no more information than necessary. This draft intentionally does not describe a particular method before engineering and legal approval. Parent/guardian contact/identity data must have its own purpose, retention and access controls.
